# Code of Conduct

Tasan is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and behavior, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information without permission.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

Report conduct concerns privately to `santeri@santeri.consulting`. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Anyone handling a report must disclose conflicts of interest and step aside when appropriate.
